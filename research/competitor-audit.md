# Competitor audit — October 11, 2026

Read accessible page text before implementation. Visual layout was not independently inspected in a browser for these competitors. Observations below concern information architecture, not a pixel-level visual audit. No art or text copied.

| Reference | Observed organization | Product lesson |
|---|---|---|
| [thehuntroblox20.com](https://thehuntroblox20.com/) | Task-first links for quests, items, rarity, years and source record; official capture dates distinguished | Put action directories and evidence near the top. A game→quest→reward relationship is more useful than a generic blog. |
| [Complete guide](https://the-hunt-roblox20.wiki/guides/complete-event-guide) | Deep topic navigation, year/game/objective/reward table and walkthrough links | Use short answers, related entity links and troubleshooting. Avoid shipping all those routes without matching evidence. |
| [Pro Game Guides event hub](https://progameguides.com/roblox-the-hunt-2026/) | Article index with named authors, publication dates and game-specific task excerpts | One page per real task intent. Our local checklist can complement article navigation. |
| [Halloween Spotlight rewards guide](https://progameguides.com/roblox/how-to-get-all-rewards-in-halloween-spotlight-roblox-event-all-keys-and-runes-guide/) | Contents list, per-game walkthrough sections, reward overview and progression explanation | Players need prerequisites and exact steps; prior-year mechanics cannot be assumed for The Hollow. |
| [The Hollow Wiki](https://roblox.fandom.com/wiki/The_Hollow) | Direct access failed | No unsupported assessment of its depth or gaps. |

## Differentiation chosen

1. Evidence labels beside the claim, with separate community watch.
2. Original dark forest/doorway artwork and crimson task directory.
3. Local-only progress, stable IDs, import/export and no account requirement.
4. Crawlable static content and empty states that explain the evidence gap.
5. Automated conservative publication rather than repeated speculative articles.

## Competition and limits

Search returned Reddit discussions, established guide sites for adjacent events, a secondary 2026 Halloween speculation article, and numerous unrelated Bleach/Hollow queries. This is a mixed-intent niche. No claim of an uncontested market is justified. No US Google page-one/page-two rank capture, keyword volume, CPC or difficulty data was available. Search-tool results are not Google US rankings.
